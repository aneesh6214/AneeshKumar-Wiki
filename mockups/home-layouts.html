Aneesh Kumar

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Aneesh Kumar is a San Francisco based computer scientist and platform engineer at Quantifind. His work spans AI infrastructure, independent software systems, and early research on emergent behavior in machine learning models.

Overview

Kumar works on practical AI systems and conceptual experiments around model reasoning, memory, and identity. He is also president of the Artificial Intelligence Club at San Francisco State University and publishes educational videos on artificial intelligence.
